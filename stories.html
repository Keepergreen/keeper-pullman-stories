The Circularity Gap Report 2026 identifies end-of-life waste as the single largest source of value loss globally — €10 trillion annually — precisely because assets are discarded before their value is exhausted. Hotels are a textbook case: high asset turnover, complex logistics, limited time to manage disposal properly.

In March 2026, we ran a full structured diversion cycle across TVs, linen, uniforms, furniture, and engineering equipment. Every item was inventoried, valued, and directed to its highest-value next use. The result was not a cost — it generated IDR 78.2M in confirmed revenue.
With Bali's tourism industry under pressure to demonstrate green credentials — and the governor publicly linking hotel sustainability to the island's future as a destination — having a documented, auditable diversion record is no longer optional. It is what Green Globe certification, CSR reporting, and guest trust increasingly require.
